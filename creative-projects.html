APEX Formula Lab.

A Formula 1 engineering playground.

A for-fun virtual engineering lab inspired by my interest in Formula 1. Explore a 3D car, experiment with vehicle setups and racing lines, and compare simulated laps through telemetry.

Launch APEX ↗View GitHub ↗

Why I made it

I wanted to explore the engineering side of Formula 1: how changes to a car’s setup and its path around a circuit can affect a simulated lap.

What you can explore

  • Inspect the car and its component hierarchy in 3D.
  • Adjust setups and experiment with racing lines.
  • Run lap simulations and review telemetry.
  • Compare configurations and experiment results.

HOW IT WAS CREATED

AI-built. Personally directed.

Astra created the site and code from my ideas, prompts, testing, and direction. My role was shaping the concept, requesting features, and iterating on the results.

This is a personal AI experiment, separate from my professional software work.

The simulation uses approximations and is intended for exploration, not validated race engineering.
